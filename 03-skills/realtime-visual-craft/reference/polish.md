# Polish — last-mile finish

Polish only after fidelity holds. Polishing a broken lighting model is expensive theater.

## Preconditions

Triple done-gate green (or explicitly waived gaps listed). Load #05 Last-Mile Craft + [[visual-qa-photoreal-rendering]].

## Steps

1. **Reconfirm gate status.** Cite still / motion / budget artifacts. If any gate red, redirect to `craft` / `harden` / `budget`.
2. **Rank residual deltas.** From last `critique` / `match`: polish-tier only (micro exposure, bloom taste, grain, tiny material response).
3. **One change at a time.** Recapture the affected pose or stress frames at native res after each meaningful tweak.
4. **Motion re-check.** Even polish can introduce crawl (dither, grain, bloom). Spot-check flythrough stress frames.
5. **Stop.** When polish-tier cues are met or diminishing returns hit, freeze. Do not invent new scope.

## Polish targets (examples)

- Highlight roll-off taste within the tonemap already chosen
- Bloom threshold / radius without changing exposure model
- Contact shadow tightness
- Material roughness micro-adjust for northstar read
- Grain / vignette that does not crawl under motion

## Do not polish by

- Adding energy-breaking fill lights
- Cranking saturation to fake "cinematic"
- Hiding aliasing with heavy TAA that ghosts under move
- Downsampling until artifacts disappear
