## For future agent
- **TL;DR:** Close-out mint covers missing QA detectors. The rest of the vault (routing, transferable processes, corollary edges, research-worthy skillsets, P0 broken graph) improves through ordinary sessions via `03-skills/self-improve/SKILL.md`. `/optimize` and `/health` still exist as periodic audits; they auto-apply mechanical P0 heals. Archive/rename/new foundation still sign-off. Git is how other devices inherit the mint.
- **As of:** 2026-09 · **Status:** current

## Context — what forced a choice
Detectors, CI, and the visual ledger already corrected and partly healed. Improve-through-use stopped at visual bugs Sean caught and QA detector mint. `/optimize` `/health` required sign-off and lived only under `.claude/skills/`, so Cursor sessions could not heal a dangling edge without paging Sean.

## Decision — what we chose
One portable skill (`self-improve`) invoked after close-out and at session-end. Three modes: correct (existing independent detector), heal (smallest broken edge), improve (smallest layer + corollary access). Transfer a *process* from another hub; do not clone its toolkit. Independent `personal-solo` push. Validity: mint only if the next cold agent on another device will load, find, or prove something they would have missed.

## Rationale — why, and what we rejected
Rejected a new numbered framework (not 3+ consumers; #08 already governs writes). Rejected promoting `/optimize` into every session (token diet). Rejected nightly inventing skills. Rejected a parallel brain.

## Consequences — what this commits us to
Agents leave a mint or an honest "no vault gap" at session-end. Hair-trigger hubs and uncalibrated detectors are still a fail. Employer wall unchanged.
