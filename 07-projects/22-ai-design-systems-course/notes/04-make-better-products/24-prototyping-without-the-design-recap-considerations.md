# Prototyping Without The Design Recap & Considerations

Anyone (QA, copy, PM, users) can prototype. **Our burden:** say when those toys are welcome, educate the org, give the CEO a path from “I made this in 30 seconds” into production-shaped work. Off-rails proto as a **handoff** into an on-rails rebuild.
