# Static Artifacts in the AI Age

Hype says traditional UI design is dead. It isn’t. Seeing is believing: napkins, wireframes, comps still explore ideas, flows, visual direction, composition. Thirty-second generation is magic; **direct manipulation** of the materials is still how you understand how a UI hangs together.

AI’s help: many variants on the table; snap fidelity (skeleton vs color) so a meeting doesn’t derail onto “that blue.” Cheap enough to sculpt live on a call.

Two modes: **off-rails** (explore new terrain) vs **on-rails** (artifact represents the org standard and can actually be built). Discern which to reach for. Demos next.
