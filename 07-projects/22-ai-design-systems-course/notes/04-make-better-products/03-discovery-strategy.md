# Discovery & Strategy

Who/what/when/where/why/how — starry-eyed and painful. Stakeholders, constraints, **context scattered** (heads, decks, wikis, hallways). DS in the plan becomes ROI tap-dance (“trust us”). AI’s job here: wrangle that context, snapshot current product, make DS value *tangible* in the plan instead of a side quest.
