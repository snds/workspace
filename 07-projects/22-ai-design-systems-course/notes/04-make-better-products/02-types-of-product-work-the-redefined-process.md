# Types of Product Work & The Redefined Process

Flavors (car metaphors): **ongoing features** (new Civic trim), **greenfield** (concept car), **legacy adoption** (electric VW bus), **retheme** (wrap). Flavor changes how you adopt the DS; process still has to run.

Industry process wars (double diamond etc.) still exist; this chapter injects AI + DS into kickoff, design, prototype-as-center, then shipping with rails. Same thesis as Ch1 “redefining digital process.”
