# Engineering Recap

Regardless of work type, engineering is the **quality threshold** before users see it. Evolution loop: DS informs products; production feeds bugs/ideas back; better system → better products. Steel curtain (tests, gates, evals) is how AI+DS stop vibe-coded crap. This is the biggest chance to strengthen the product↔system loop.
