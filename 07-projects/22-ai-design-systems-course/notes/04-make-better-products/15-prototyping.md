# Prototyping

“A prototype is worth 1,000 meetings” used to mean specialists and expense. Natural language now democratizes working prototypes — interactivity, state, animation, performance, in the environment the product will live. Double-edged. Split: **without** the DS (most vibe-coded stuff) vs **on the rails** of the org standard.
