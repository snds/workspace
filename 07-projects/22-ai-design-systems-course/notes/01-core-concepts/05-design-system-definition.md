# Design system definition

They survey Salesforce, IxDF, NN/g, Figma — all “standards + reusable bits + consistency.” Brad’s preferred line:

**A design system is critical UI infrastructure. It is the story of how your organization designs and builds digital interfaces.**

People used to be annoyed that this isn’t “components and tokens.” He wants the narrative/shared-agreement layer. Now that LLMs can do a lot of the mechanical bits, the remaining question is: *what story are we telling the machines about how we do UI here?*

## For Sean

Maps cleanly onto “rationale is the artifact” and component contracts: the system is not the Figma file, it’s the arbitrable story. LLMs make the storytelling layer *more* important, not less — they will happily invent a different story if you don’t give them yours.
