# AI for Digital Product Work

Capability list, not a method: generate (design, code, images, prototypes, copy, data); iterate/fix/refine/ideate; translate across environments and modalities; analyze (research, code, design, content); plan (break into steps); execute via agents (auto or semi).

That’s why leadership is yelling “use AI.” The next lessons split value from hard.
