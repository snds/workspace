# A Brief History of AI

They disclaim: not AI researchers. Grain of salt; books + course resource hub at the end.

## Timeline they want you to have

- 1950 Turing — “Can machines think?” / imitation game. Still the feel of chatting with a model.
- 1956 Dartmouth coins “artificial intelligence,” then AI winters (70s–80s).
- 1986 Rumelhart / Hinton / Williams — backprop, neural nets that *learn*.
- 2012 AlexNet blows out ImageNet → deep learning as the path; DeepMind → Google (2014); OpenAI founded 2015 (nonprofit, AGI-for-humanity mission; capability vs safety vs openness tension from day one).
- AlphaGo Move 37 — not just imitation; “creative” play.
- **2017 “Attention Is All You Need”** — transformers; look at all tokens at once. The T in GPT. All modern LLMs descend from this.
- GPT-3 (175B params, web/Wikipedia/books) — surprising code/reason-like behavior. OpenAI splits: ship/commercialize (Altman, Microsoft money) vs safety. 2021 Amodeis leave → Anthropic.
- 2021 Copilot (first daily-driver AI for millions of devs); DALL·E.
- **30 Nov 2022 ChatGPT** — 100M users in two months; mainstream.
- 2023 Claude, GPT-4, Bard → Gemini.
- 2024 agents doing work, not just chat; Anthropic **MCP**.
- 2025 agentic tools in the workflow (Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf). Recording-week name-drops: Claude Design, GPT Image 2. Pace does not slow.

## For Sean

Keep the OpenAI/Anthropic split as context for why this course’s stack leans Claude/Cursor/MCP. The only load-bearing technical beat is **transformers + MCP + agents in the IDE**. The rest is orientation.
