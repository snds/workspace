# Chapter 1 Introduction

Agenda for the chapter, then they immediately zoom out to *why DS exists*.

## Chapter shape

1. Whirlwind history: technology → computing → UI → design systems → AI (this moment)
2. DS core: evolving definition, benefits, pain points
3. Same pass for AI: definition, benefits, pain points
4. The combination: quality + scale + speed
5. Terminology / tools so later chapters share a vocabulary

Spirit of the chapter: **alignment and shared understanding** before the weeds.

## Framing they sneak in here

Digital product work is hard because the job is: ship UI onto billions of connected rectangles, now also teaching the machines to act on their own, **while keeping some control over what goes out**. Design systems are what they reach for as that control surface.

Mustafa Suleyman (*The Coming Wave*): AI as a **general-purpose technology** (steam, semiconductors) — a new epoch, not a feature.
