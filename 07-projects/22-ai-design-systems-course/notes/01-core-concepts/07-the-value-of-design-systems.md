# The value of design systems

Without a system: inconsistent, low-quality, inaccessible UI; every surface a first draft; nothing connected; a maintenance nightmare. Everyone pays — users, makers, the org. That’s an expensive problem.

With a system: cohesive accessible UX, time spent on higher-value work (not the 70th button), connected source of truth, rebrands/big swings become tractable.

They explicitly pitch this as **more valuable in the agentic age**, not less.

## For Sean

Use this as the stakeholder one-liner when someone asks why not just vibe-code screens: without the system, agents multiply first drafts.
