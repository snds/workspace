# What's Hard About AI?

Pair with [[08-what-s-hard-about-design-systems]]. This is the other half of the problem statement.

## Technology

- **Non-deterministic.** Same input ≠ same output. You traded reliability for superpowers.
- **Hallucinations**, trained on a biased internet, delivered with **certitude** and **sycophancy**. Harmful brew.
- Cost: money (vendors want ROI) and non-money (carbon, water, data centers). Macro hards get a pointer, not a deep dive.

## Quality of results

Uneven and often *unknown*. Looks-good ≠ is-good, across brand, UX, visual, a11y, FE/BE, security, privacy. Even if it passes first glance: is it durable or fragile?

## People

No prior art for the paradigm. Humans are bad at breakneck adaptation. Job-market disruption. Pressure cooker → wellbeing. Direct ask: **be kind to yourself and others**; others may be overwhelmed even if you’re fine.

## Process / org

Buy-in, sensible adoption, safe/respectful/responsible/secure scale. Governing a moving target.

Close: DS+AI is the rest of the course, but don’t ignore the broader issues. Quote Sustainable UX Network: **no good UX on a dead planet.**

## For Sean

Non-determinism + sycophancy is why this workspace insists on contracts, Proofboard, and human taste as the done-gate. Kindness/wellbeing is worth keeping in stakeholder comms; don’t let it substitute for the quality mechanism.
