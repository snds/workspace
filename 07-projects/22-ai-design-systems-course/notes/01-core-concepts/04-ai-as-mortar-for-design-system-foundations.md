# AI as mortar for design system foundations

The history lesson’s point: the connected graph is **hard-earned and fragile**. AI’s job is not to replace it. AI is **mortar** — it oozes into the cracks between already-built bricks (people, process, design libs, code libs, docs) and makes the foundation sturdier.

They insist the history dump matters because *where assets live, how they connect, and whether those connections are real* is the actual gig of managing UI at an org. Set the system up “just so” or the mortar has nothing to bind.
