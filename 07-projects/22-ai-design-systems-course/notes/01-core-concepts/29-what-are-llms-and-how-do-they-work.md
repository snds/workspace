# What are LLMs and how do they work?

Trained on massive text (web, blogs, SO, Reddit, images…) to generate language. They learn **patterns**, not facts from a DB. Next-token prediction; billions of parameters.

Good at: writing, summarizing, translating, Q&A/explain, generating/reviewing code, multi-step reasoning (they flag early hallucination risk; still recommend it now).
