# How AI Can Help With Assessing A Design System

Pop the hood. Manual DS assessment is what they’ve done for a decade and it’s slow, siloed, expertise-uneven, and **stale the day after**. Paper cuts in the cracks become engine failure.

AI: crawl many assets faster; score against org standard *and* industry practice; surface drift/gaps/contradictions humans miss; most valuable on **relationships between** design, code, and docs — then turn the corner into fixes. Still needs a method (next lessons), not a vibe prompt.
