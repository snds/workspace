# The Check Engine Light For Design Systems

System *seems* fine (ships, gets used) but the light is on — loose gas cap vs transmission.

- **Gas cap:** inconsistent layer names, off-token colors, components that look right but break rules. Cosmetic, tedious, hard without tooling.
- **Alternator:** detached instances, hardcoded values, missing props. Still runs; structure eroding.
- **Transmission:** no semantic structure, no metadata, no connection to code. AI then generates garbage and does more harm than good.
