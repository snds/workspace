# Station 8: Feedback & Adoption

Track adoption **and** whether product work feeds the system. Light-on: no metrics; “this is broke please fix”; DS team detached from product roadmaps; discovering after the fact that teams scrapped/hacked the system. Tooling has been evasive. AI as mortar: watch product files/repos/backlogs/roadmaps, then connect product management and DS backlog.
