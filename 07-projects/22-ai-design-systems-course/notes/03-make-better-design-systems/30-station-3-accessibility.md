# Station 3: Accessibility

Own station because it matters. Light-on: lawsuits/fear; no or ad-hoc testing; contrast; missing keyboard; no component-level *or* high-level a11y docs. Cultural: “we’re moving fast.” Lighthouse/Axe help; real user feedback matters more.

AI: audit then fix (e.g. retune tokens for WCAG). Still a selling lens for DS to the org (Intro constitution).
