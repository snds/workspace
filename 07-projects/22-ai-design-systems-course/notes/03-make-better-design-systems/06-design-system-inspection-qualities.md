# Design System Inspection Qualities

Car multi-point inspection. Five qualities (first four are just a good DS; fifth is the AI overlay):

1. **Complete** — exists in the formats the org needs; tokens, components, variants, docs enough for product work.
2. **Sound** — actually good: industry + org quality, a11y, consistent, tested.
3. **Synchronized** — assets connected; design/code/docs embodied in the *workflow*, not just files.
4. **Extensible** — reliable evolution; governance; adoption measured; product work feeds the system (virtuous cycle).
5. **AI-ready** — those ingredients are available to agents so LLMs can wield them in product work.
