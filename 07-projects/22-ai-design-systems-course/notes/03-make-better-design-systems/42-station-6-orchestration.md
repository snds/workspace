# Station 6: Orchestration

Are assets connected, and are **workflows** synchronized? Pre-AI’s biggest DS pain. Drift: Figma vs code language, repo vs published package, stale docs. Usually **handoff energy** instead of cross-disciplinary work.

AI can assess assets *and* human process: meeting notes, Jira/GitHub/Notion, email/Slack. Cold shower, then feed that into sturdier process. TJ’s **context-based DS** is the method.
