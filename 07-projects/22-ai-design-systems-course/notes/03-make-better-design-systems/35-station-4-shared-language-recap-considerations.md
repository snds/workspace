# Station 4: Shared Language Recap & Considerations

Language is the **contract** — glue for humans and the thing AI reasons with. Don’t be a bull in a china shop on production props/tokens; use versioning, codemods, communication. Goal: maintainable system that’s easier for consumers **and** agents.
