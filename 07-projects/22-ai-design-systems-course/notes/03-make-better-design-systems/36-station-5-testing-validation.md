# Station 5: Testing & Validation

Architecture for ongoing quality. Light-on: no CI/tests/lint (local vibes → silent bugs); no visual regression; tests that go through the motions; **no evals** on AI-produced code. AI: coverage report then fill holes. Code-library-heavy station. Grain of salt: quality-in-nondeterminism is unfinished industry work.
