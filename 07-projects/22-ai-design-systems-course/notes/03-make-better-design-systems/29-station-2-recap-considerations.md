# Station 2: Recap & Considerations

“Best practices” = industry conventions + tool/format conventions (Figma, code architecture) + **org** brand/UX/FE standards. Assess with full-stool context; **fix** one asset at a time. Goal: downstream teams get those practices for free when they plug the system in.
