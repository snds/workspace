# The Future is Wide Open Recap & Considerations

Frontier is emerging *and* already here. Stay curious; **protect the roadmap** from shiny bait. Same for personal/team mental health — capacity has limits. Almost nothing is all-or-nothing (LinkedIn “this is dead” is wrong); new techniques combine with old (try it on the hero; leave the rest). Oversaturated; people beg to pump the brakes; pace still grows as more people can actualize ideas. Talk openly about risks / known unknowns / unknown unknowns. Venture **cautiously**.
