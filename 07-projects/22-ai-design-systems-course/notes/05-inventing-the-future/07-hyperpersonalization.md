# Hyperpersonalization

Old story: pros ship an experience; people use it as-is. Classic “personalization” was modest (history, location). Real humans carry language, culture, unique life, mood — products barely touch that. Accessibility is the gut-punch: “blind people aren’t our target audience,” or even well-intentioned orgs can’t account for every ability. **AI can assemble that context at the moment of use.**

DS is the rail under the fluidity: high personalization without leaving the catalog. **Tokens as handshake** between user aesthetic prefs and system foundations (tokens used to be for brands; now users bring their own). Superposition: design spells possibilities; the user arrives with a specific context; they meet in the middle. Especially excited about a11y: sturdy foundations + individual needs.

**For Sean:** hyperpersonalization is not a one-off theme file — it’s tokens + gen UI on the same blessed catalog.
