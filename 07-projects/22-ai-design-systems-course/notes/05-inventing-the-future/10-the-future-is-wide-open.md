# The Future is Wide Open

Frontier bucket (Tom Petty included). Themes queued: **realtime UI** (speak → text → LLM → talk to a UI; extra inputs on gen UI), **diffusion** into UX/UI, people **rolling their own software** and foregoing org products, **creative infinite** (anyone create/combine/remix on demand, forever). Rumsfeld: known knowns / known unknowns / unknown unknowns. Novelty vs stability — DS is solid ground *and* creative raw materials (tokens hooked to tech that didn’t exist yesterday). Triple threat: infrastructure + remix + whatever’s next. Demos follow.
