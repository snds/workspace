# Chapter 5 Summary, Homework, and Next Steps

UX: fixed → fluid/generative. Agents join humans. Gen UI composes DS at runtime. Hyperpersonalization considers abilities/context/prefs. Creative infinite: anyone who can speak can create → **quality and care** become the job. Slack community is the live lab.

**Homework:** discuss agents as first-class users in kickoff/strategy/design/dev. Experiment on a **small slice** of product (gen UI / hyperpersonalization). Sanctioned play, not instead of meat-and-potatoes. Show-and-tell at biweekly jams. Keep up via Slack + resources hub.
