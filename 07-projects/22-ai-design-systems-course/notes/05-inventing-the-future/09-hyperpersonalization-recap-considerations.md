# Hyperpersonalization Recap & Considerations

Same spirit as gen UI: DS is the guardrail while AI reshapes from context/prefs/abilities. Dream: **self-expression + digital quality** coexist (MySpace skins vs Facebook’s singular UX). Risks: filter bubbles, bias, deceptive patterns, exploitation as people bring more context. Puzzle: richer UX without a tighter bubble. Answer he wants: **control, choice, agency, visibility** into how the experience is shifting. More context → **proportionally more commitment to safety**.
