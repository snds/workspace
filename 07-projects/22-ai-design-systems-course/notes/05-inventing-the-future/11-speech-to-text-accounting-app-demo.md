# Speech-To-Text Accounting App Demo

Stacking modalities: browser **speech recognition** (Chromium-only at recording; mic → Google transformer → words back). Fictitious accounting app; robot button opens Web Audio. Hands-free: ADHD focus (chrome fades), spend-on-software this year vs last, download statements, email accountant, check last expense, **rebrand** + **dark mode** as keyword latches — same as a boolean toggle, not a full regen.

Speech → text is the unlock; after that you can map keywords, query an LLM, show/hide, reorder. Tokens make theme-switch a one-word command. Gen UI optional. Homework: talk with the team about speech (and other APIs) as product, then share experiments.

**For Sean:** hyperpersonalization here is architecture (tokens + keyword map) plus a cheap input, not a new surface for every ability.
