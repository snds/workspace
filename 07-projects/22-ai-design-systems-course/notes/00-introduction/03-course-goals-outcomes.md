# Course Goals & Outcomes

## Claims

- The landscape is hype + snake oil + shifting sand. The course is trying to be **solid ground**, not another tool tour.
- Core move: use DS + AI so products are built **with the grain of the organization's standard**, not from whatever materials the model conjures.
- They promise **worked implementations** (basic → advanced), not slides about possibility.
- Success looks like **collaborative workflows** across design, development, product, and other stakeholders — not a designer-only Copilot trick.
- They will keep updating as the stack moves; students are meant to learn *with* them.
