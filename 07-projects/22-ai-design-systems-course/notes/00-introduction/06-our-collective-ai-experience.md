# Our Collective AI Experience

## Claims

- They entered AI **through design systems work**, not the other way around. ChatGPT's public arrival was the catalyst (~3 years before recording).
- Early-era tools: lots of hype that "culminated into nothing." The filter was what survived in **large-org, large-system** maintenance — not demo-ware.
- Even now it "still feels like the Big Bang." Best practices and hard truths are scarce. They claim some "clouds of gas and rock" are coalescing into an approach.

## For Sean

Useful humility check: treat this course as **field notes from practitioners who ship DS at scale**, not as a settled canon. Weigh their coalescing practices against workspace doctrine (contracts, QA operating model, token grain) instead of adopting tool choices wholesale.
