# Who This Course Is For

## Claims

- Audience is anyone invested in digital-product success — not a designer-only course.
- Design systems are framed as the **watering hole**: the shared language that keeps silos aligned while products are designed, built, launched, maintained, grown.
- Special attention to the **designer ↔ developer** relationship; also meant to pull non-technical stakeholders closer.
- They will not water it down. Crawl → walk → run, independent of your DS maturity or AI maturity.

## For Sean

You are the primary audience they named (in-the-weeds DS designer who also lives in code/Figma plugins). The "watering hole" metaphor is useful when talking to Centric stakeholders: the DS is not a component catalog, it's the alignment surface. Don't let later tool chapters (Cursor, Figma MCP, Story UI) collapse that back into a designer-solo workflow.
