# Piloting AI & Design Systems

Sell ↔ pilot is a **blurry line** — the pilot *is* often the pitch. Build for real: DS success attached to **product success** (checkout rebuild, homepage), not a side generated library you then try to shop. Latch the system’s fate to a live workstream. Shrink scope so you can learn without making a giant mess (same instinct as context-based DS).

**How:** product-inspection baseline → after picture (adopted, on time, on budget). Quick wins; don’t boil the ocean; PM milestones / user stories = places to decide AI vs human. On-rails prototypes as disposable “does this feel right?” journeys (Zoom/share, toss, add the expired-card path). Iteration isn’t free (tokens) but beats canvas-only. Fail a lot; spoonfeed context; 3% then 5% then 10%. Multiplayer jams (FigJam → DS prototypes) compound wins.

**New vs old pilots:** not “ship and sunset.” Eddie **reporter** watches downstream product (GitHub issues as signal) and feeds the system. Stack 2–5 quick wins so it isn’t an anomaly. Those loops are the on-ramp to rollout across products/stacks.

**For Sean:** Centric pilot = one real product workstream + inspection baseline + reporter-style feedback, not a demo DS.
