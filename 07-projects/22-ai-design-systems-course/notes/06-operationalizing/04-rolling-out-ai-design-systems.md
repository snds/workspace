# Rolling Out AI & Design Systems

After enough pilot wins, make it **roadmap + org standard**, not more sandboxes. Orgs fumble here on **communication, guidelines, governance**. Pilot = skunkworks pod that fails until a method works; **document failures/passes into markdown** (human + eventually machine-readable) so the next team inherits rails.

Steel curtain / CI is how DS spirit stops being goodwill-and-vibes: products are **forbidden from rolling their own** by default. Scale a pilot — not a light switch. Skeptics and weirder use cases arrive at rollout. Prove with measured pass/fail, not concept. Political arc: often CTO/CPO with data, not fist-fighting every PO.

Diversity (products, users, tools, models) is DS input: accommodate vs consolidate? Guide = real humans (checkout ≠ internal app). Peer PM-to-PM: inform, don’t pitch — “this worked for us, take fragments.” Mid-pilot transparency. AI as **mortar between people** (mental models, overloaded teams): spell feelings out, map to infrastructure, evolve the system — not yes/no battle. Supercharge human understanding; still needs facilitation. “Here’s how this impacts you / what changes / how the system changes for you.”
