# Chapter 6 Summary, Homework, and Next Steps

Arc recap: sandbox experiments **sell** (shout-out Lou / Waypoint DS) → rightsized **pilot** on real planned-not-started work with enthusiastic guinea pigs → stack wins + hooks → **rollout** into diversity/skepticism → **govern** via real system↔product connection. Stay sane; evaluate trends without taking every bait. Care for people; responsible use.

**Homework:** name where you are (sell / pilot / rollout). Especially in sales: who must be sold, what they need to hear, what success/failure looks like *for them*. Pick pilots on the calendar that haven’t started. Copy how your org already rolls out rebrands/replatforms. Put the feedback loop in **early**. Guardrails for wellbeing; put AI in its place.
