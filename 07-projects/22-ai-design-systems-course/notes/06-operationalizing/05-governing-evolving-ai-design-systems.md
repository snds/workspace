# Governing & Evolving AI & Design Systems

Governance starts when the **first pilot still exists** — checkout team keeps shipping and poking the DS. Old model: NPM install, Slack yell, weak “it’s broken” tickets. New: **dual-filed, shaped, cross-linked issues** that auto-close when the DS version ships. “Less sexy gap” is product↔system, not design↔dev.

Mortar is not only AI: GitHub hooks, multi-repo Eddie reporting, Linear/Jira MCP tickets with full system history. **Cron product-inspection** (Lighthouse-like 0–100) → adoption/a11y regressions as infrared. Landscape pace: privacy/security re-eval; token budgets (moodboard UI isn’t free); don’t optimize the wrong loop; jam partners keep hill-climbs focused.

New tools must **earn a place** in infrastructure connected to the whole product landscape — wedge against 17 tools while you just stood one up. Take care of yourself: boundaries at person/team/DS/org/society. Curated hour of reading, not ping-on-every-model. You aren’t behind. Community is the health infrastructure.
