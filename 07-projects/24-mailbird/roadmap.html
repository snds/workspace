Waiting on you

  • The product name #"Mailbird" belongs to Mailbird, Inc. (ADR-0006). Google's app verification is blocked until it changes.
    What this means

    “Mailbird” is Mailbird, Inc.’s trademark, so the project can’t ship under it. Google won’t verify the sign-in app until the name is final, and the name goes into the app id, the website and the OAuth consent screen. You pick a name; I rename the app and docs in one pass.

  • A Google OAuth client #For real Gmail and Google sign-ins. Steps are in docs/google-verification.md.
    What this means

    Real Gmail and Google Calendar sign-ins need an OAuth client in your Google Cloud account; I can’t create one. It’s free for testing (up to 100 test users) and needs Google’s verification to go public, which waits on the name. You follow docs/google-verification.md and paste me the client id.

  • A Microsoft Entra app #For real Outlook and Microsoft 365 sign-ins. Steps are in docs/microsoft-setup.md.
    What this means

    Real Outlook and Microsoft 365 sign-ins need an app registration in Microsoft Entra; I can’t create one. It’s free and takes about ten minutes. You follow docs/microsoft-setup.md and paste me the client id.

  • A desktop check of the menu-bar calendar (42) #The tray icon, its window, and keep-running-on-close need macOS, Windows, and Linux. ADR-0054 has a 10-row checklist.
    What this means

    The tray icon, its mini window and keep-running-on-close only exist in a real desktop window, which the cloud sessions can’t open. On each of your Macs and the Windows PC, you run the app and go through ADR-0054’s 10-row checklist; I fix whatever fails.

  • Real phones for the device spike (5b) #iOS and Android builds, location reminders, and Pencil handwriting can only be checked on hardware.
    What this means

    iOS and Android builds, location reminders, Apple Pencil, the native scanners, and copying and saving on a phone can only be checked on a device. You’ll need your iPhone (and an Android phone if you have one) and an Apple developer account for the iOS build (a free one installs on your own phone; TestFlight needs the paid one). We do it together in one session.
